# Hackathon preparation

Planning date: October 26, 2026. Official rules and participant portal take priority over this checklist.

The [official Meta event page](https://dev.meta.ai/events/global-hackathon) was checked during preparation. It lists the Indie and Startup tracks, the themes Build for Your World and Push the Frontier, and submission by November 4 at 21:00 Eastern. That is 18:00 Pacific on November 4. Dates may change; check again before submission.

## Proposed positioning

Build for Your World; Indie track if that matches the participant's registration and eligibility. Core contribution: an agentic capture-to-retest loop. Show actual Meta use in the working path. Do not rely on an attractive dashboard as the contribution.

## Confirm in the participant portal

- Current eligibility, team registration and permitted reuse of previous work.
- Public-repository and video requirements, maximum duration and accepted hosts.
- Exact submission fields and whether a hosted demo is required.
- Model eligibility and any bonus-specific verification conditions.
- Data, licensing and publication terms.

Earlier TacitOS notes report a public repository and a three-minute video requirement from kickoff material. These are planning inputs inherited from those notes; the private participant portal was not accessed for this pack. No rubric weights or prize claims are necessary to the concept.

## Submission readiness

- [ ] Implemented behavior matches the description.
- [ ] Meta model use and actual model ID are documented.
- [ ] Synthetic actors, data and replay are labelled.
- [ ] Evaluation results have denominators and limitations.
- [ ] Source and assets are publishable; secrets and runtime data are excluded.
- [ ] A clean setup can reproduce the demo.
- [ ] Repository and video links work with the required access settings.
- [ ] Submission confirmation is saved before the deadline.
