# Research and novelty assessment

Reviewed October 26, 2026. This is a bounded search, not an exhaustive market or patent review. Vendor descriptions are evidence of advertised positioning, not independently verified performance. No market-size or revenue claims are inferred.

## Relevant precedents

- [Botminds Expert Knowledge Capture](https://www.botminds.ai/solutions/expert-knowledge-capture) describes capturing expert interviews and walk-throughs with approved, cited responses. This overlaps with capture and retrieval, so those alone are not our novelty claim.
- [Kairos by trophi.ai](https://www.trophi.ai/kairos/) describes expert-built experiential training and AI-supported instruction. Generic expert-to-simulator authoring is therefore not a sufficient differentiator.
- [Microsoft Machine Teaching](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/group/machine-teaching-group/) describes interactive teaching by subject-matter experts. Learning from corrections is established territory.
- [Counterfactual Active Learning, ACL 2023](https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.636/) studies selecting factual and counterfactual examples. Generating boundary cases is not, on its own, a new scientific invention.
- [Vacation Simulator](https://brittonbroderick.com/2021/11/14/chaos-engineering-with-the-vacation-simulator/) describes deliberately rehearsing an employee's unavailability and tracking interruptions. This is a direct conceptual precedent for absence exercises.
- [Simulation-based knowledge elicitation](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0957417412001984) reports research involving a manufacturing simulation. Simulation as a capture method predates this project.

## Our proposed distinction

Connect an observed task dependency to a narrow expert intervention and then to an independently graded fresh transfer attempt, with versioned evidence throughout. The target outcome is demonstrated task continuity within a stated scope. The hypothesis is that AI makes this closed loop affordable and repeatable for teams that would otherwise rely on informal handovers.

This is a product and workflow hypothesis. We have not established that it is unique, that customers will pay, or that it outperforms a competent human-led handover.

## Evidence needed next

A working prototype establishes technical feasibility. Equal-information comparisons test whether approved guidance adds value beyond raw answers. Equal-expert-time comparisons test the capture workflow. A real successor study tests transfer beyond synthetic model behavior. Customer adoption and willingness to pay test the commercial claim.

## Language to use

“Rehearses a handover and tests task-level transfer.” “Designed to expose undocumented dependencies.” “Transfer demonstrated on these cases.”

## Language to avoid

“First ever.” “All expertise captured.” “Guaranteed business continuity.” “This employee is replaceable.” “GDPR compliant by design.” “Proven disruptive.” None is supported by this pack.
