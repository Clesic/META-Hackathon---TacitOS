## Name

Missing Monday

## One sentence

Missing Monday rehearses an experienced employee's absence, captures the knowledge that blocks the team, and tests whether a successor can now do the work.

## Problem

Organizations often discover undocumented dependencies only when an experienced employee is unavailable. A handbook or recorded interview does not show whether the next person can handle the exceptions that matter.

## Proposed solution

A successor attempts representative tasks in a safe rehearsal. When work stalls, Missing Monday identifies the missing distinction and asks the expert a focused question. It turns the answer into expert-approved guidance, then tests transfer on fresh cases. The result is evidence of which tasks can be handed over and which still require help.

## Intended demo

One synthetic purchasing workflow with undocumented supplier exceptions. The demo follows a blockage through expert capture, review, a successful fresh case and an important boundary case. Results will be reported only after the working system is evaluated.

## Intended Meta use

A Meta model will diagnose gaps, ask context-specific questions and extract conditional guidance. Optional speech transcription will be added only if the core loop is stable. The final submission must name the models actually used and describe any failures or fallbacks.

## Differentiation

The proposed contribution is a closed loop from a demonstrated dependency to a tested handover. Interviews, simulation and absence exercises have precedents; we do not claim to have invented them. The project tests whether AI can make that complete loop practical for small teams.

## Honest limitations

The initial cases are synthetic and cover one narrow workflow. Passing them does not certify an entire role or prove real-world business impact. The final submission must distinguish implemented features, simulations and future plans.
